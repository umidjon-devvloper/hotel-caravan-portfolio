About the hotel

Comfort and tradition in the heart of Bukhara


Caravan Plaza is located in the historic centre of Bukhara, on Khodja Nurobobod Street. The hotel combines modern comforts with traditional Uzbek hospitality.

Rooms have air conditioning, central heating, a private bathroom, a king-size bed, a safe and a writing desk. A telephone, television, free Wi-Fi and other modern conveniences are provided for guests.

Bathrooms are equipped with a shower, bath, hairdryer, bathrobe and toiletries. Family rooms and non-smoking rooms are available.
