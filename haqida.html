About the hotel

Comfort and tradition in the heart of Bukhara

Hotel Caravan is located in the historic centre of Bukhara, on Khodja Nurobobod Street. The hotel combines modern comforts with traditional Uzbek hospitality.

Rooms have air conditioning, central heating, a private bathroom, a king-size bed, a safe and a writing desk. A telephone, television, free Wi-Fi and other modern conveniences are provided for guests.

Bathrooms are equipped with a shower, bath, hairdryer, bathrobe and toiletries. Family rooms and non-smoking rooms are available.

Location

In the centre of Bukhara

The hotel is located in the historic centre of Bukhara, close to the famous Lyabi-Hauz complex. Ancient madrasas and mosques, bazaars and museums are all nearby.

There are convenient transport connections to the airport and the railway station. An ideal base for exploring the city.
